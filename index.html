Hybrid

Strength & Conditioning · 45 mins

Our hybrid class combines both strength training and cardiovascular exercise, so you’re not neglecting any aspect of fitness. The cardio elements keep your heart rate elevated, improving cardiovascular endurance and burning calories, while the strength exercises help build muscle, increase power, and improve overall body strength.

By combining both training styles in one session, participants can improve their overall fitness, endurance, muscle tone, and cardiovascular health at the same time. This makes hybrid classes an efficient and effective way to achieve a balanced level of fitness.

Sweat

Strength & Conditioning · 30–35 mins

Our Sweat class is a high-intensity, 30–35 minute workout of constant work, designed to get your heart rate up and leave you feeling energised. You’ll move continuously through a series of stations, including boxing bag intervals to develop power, coordination and cardiovascular fitness, ERG machines to build endurance, bodyweight exercises, and free weights to improve strength and muscular endurance.

With minimal rest between stations, the class keeps the intensity high, helping you burn calories, boost fitness, and start your day with a great sweat.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience to start?

None at all. Every class welcomes complete beginners — our Beginners & Intermediates BJJ sessions, Muay Thai classes and all S&C classes are coached for every level, and you’ll never be thrown in at the deep end.

How do I claim my free first session?

Email us, message us on Instagram, or just walk in. Any class, any discipline — your first one is on us, with no card details and no commitment.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable sportswear and a water bottle. For BJJ, a gi if you have one — no-gi classes just need shorts and a t-shirt or rash guard. Don’t worry about kit for your first session; come as you are.

Are there contracts?

No. Every membership is rolling monthly and you can cancel anytime. The Founding 50 rate is a reward, not a lock-in — it simply holds your lower price for your first year.

What does “off-peak gym use” mean?

The Ultimate members can use the gym floor and facilities for their own training throughout our opening hours, outside of class times. The 12-Class Pass and Single Discipline Unlimited cover classes only.

What ages are the kids classes?

Kids BJJ runs in two groups — ages 4–11 and 12–15 — alongside kids Muay Thai. All kids classes begin after the six-week school holidays, from 1st September.

Can I just pay for one class?

Yes — walk-ins are £15 a session, no membership needed. It’s a great way to try a few different classes before picking a plan.

Is there parking?

Yes — we offer on-site parking for the convenience of our members and visitors, at Unit 7 Twyford Road, Bishop Stortford CM23 3LJ.
