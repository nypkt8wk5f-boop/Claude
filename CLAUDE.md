# Working rules for Ultimate Fight Fitness

## Replies to members, parents and enquiries
- Keep every reply short, kind, simple and polite. A few lines, not a page.
- Only state things Ryan has confirmed. Never invent logistics or procedures.
- There is NO sign-in form, NO registration form and NO online booking needed for a first session. People just turn up.
- Never tell people to bring a water bottle.
- Never say "no kit needed". For No-Gi classes say shorts or leggings and a t-shirt.
- BJJ newcomers: always point them to a No-Gi class, because it only needs shorts and a t-shirt. Add that if they want to do a Gi class, they should buy a gi online. Do not say they can start a Gi class in a t-shirt and shorts. (Ryan, 16 November 2026.)
- First session is always free.
- Never ask people to arrive early. No "get here 10 to 15 minutes early", in any wording. (Ryan, 16 November 2026.)
- Kids classes are ages 4 to 15. Kids BJJ Tuesday 4.30pm (Gi) and Thursday 4.30pm (No-Gi). Kids Muay Thai Tuesday 5.30pm and Saturday 10am.
- Prices only when asked: £10 a kids class, £59.99 a month Kids Unlimited, £15 adult walk-in, £10 open mat walk-in.
- Sign-off: never add a signature. No name, gym name or phone number. End with just "Thanks," or "Best regards," and nothing after it, because Ryan's email signature is added automatically. This applies to every email, including consultants and the council. (Ryan, 16 November 2026.)

## Tone everywhere else
- Social captions: punchy, human, no cringe, no hard sell.
- Council and consultants: calm, factual, dated, civil.
- If unsure of a fact, ask Ryan. Do not fill gaps with assumptions, especially dates.

## ClubWise how-tos
These come from ClubWise support emails. Use them as written and do not guess at steps that are not listed.
- Freeze a member: member's status tab > freeze > freeze membership > select the dates. Tick "Entry permitted" if they can still come in. (Lewis Woolman, 24 September 2026.)
- Upgrade or move a member: member profile > update membership status > membership status > select the new membership > remove the pro rata and joining fee > confirm and continue > complete the sale. (Muzaffar Ali, 27 October 2026.)
- Change a membership's fee for new joiners: Back > Setup > POS > Billed Memberships for Sale > select the membership > Membership tab > amend the fee > Save. Existing members keep their old fee until each one is moved using the upgrade steps. (26 October 2026.)
- See current members: Back menu > dashboard > memberships tab > DD memberships. For a report: Back menu > reports > current > run as a CSV.
- Cut-off: direct debits for the 1st are processed 12 days before the end of the month. A change made after that misses the next payment. ClubWise's advice then is to let the payment go out and refund it. Money reaches the gym 5 working days after the 1st.
- Memberships cannot be renamed or deleted, only hidden from the POS. ClubWise creates new ones on request.
- Cancelling a membership: the exact clicks are not recorded yet. Ruby processed one on 18 November 2026 (status "Set to term", with a termination receipt), so she knows the steps. Ask her, then record them here. A cancellation record shows the date the member gave notice, the final billing date, the membership end date and the reason.
