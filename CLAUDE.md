# Working rules for Ultimate Fight Fitness

## Replies to members, parents and enquiries
- Keep every reply short, kind, simple and polite. A few lines, not a page.
- Only state things Ryan has confirmed. Never invent logistics or procedures.
- There is NO sign-in form, NO registration form and NO online booking needed for a first session. People just turn up.
- Never tell people to bring a water bottle.
- Never say "no kit needed". For No-Gi classes say shorts or leggings and a t-shirt. For Gi classes say they can start in a t-shirt and shorts.
- First session is always free. Arriving 10 to 15 minutes early to meet the coach is fine to suggest.
- Kids classes are ages 4 to 15. Kids BJJ Tuesday 4.30pm (Gi) and Thursday 4.30pm (No-Gi). Kids Muay Thai Tuesday 5.30pm and Saturday 10am.
- Prices only when asked: £10 a kids class, £59.99 a month Kids Unlimited, £15 adult walk-in, £10 open mat walk-in.
- Sign-off: Ryan, Ultimate Fight Fitness, 07885 521508.

## Tone everywhere else
- Social captions: punchy, human, no cringe, no hard sell.
- Council and consultants: calm, factual, dated, civil.
- If unsure of a fact, ask Ryan. Do not fill gaps with assumptions, especially dates.
